littesecret Shorts Uploader

An internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel littesecret. It automatically creates short, educational videos for children (in English and Turkish) and uploads them to that channel.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

  • Who uses it: Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • Google data accessed: The tool requests only the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos to the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect or store any other YouTube or Google account data.
  • Viewer data: The tool collects no information about viewers. All uploaded videos are marked "made for kids".
  • Storage and sharing: The OAuth credential is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Revoking access: Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Contact: littiinfo832@gmail.com

Gizlilik (Türkçe özet)

Bu araç yalnızca littesecret kanalının sahibi tarafından, kendi kanalına çocuklara yönelik eğitici kısa videolar yüklemek için kullanılır. Sadece video yükleme izni ister; başka hiçbir hesap veya izleyici verisi toplamaz, saklamaz ya da paylaşmaz. İzin istenildiği zaman myaccount.google.com/permissions adresinden kaldırılabilir.
